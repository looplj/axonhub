### Actual owner note

The transformer guideline's “Responses Body Field Storage” wording assigns several fields broadly to `ProviderExtensions.OpenAIResponses.Request`, while source currently stores `background`, `include`, `max_tool_calls`, `prompt_cache_retention`, and `truncation` in `TransformerMetadata`; only raw nested `stream_options` is in `OpenAIResponsesRequestExtensions`. This is a `DOC_ONLY` evidence-alignment issue, not proof that behavior is broken and not a reason to widen `llm.Request`.
